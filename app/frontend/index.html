Personalized Financial Planning
From bank SMS to an explained savings plan

Whose money are we planning?

The forecaster learns from spending history, so the demo starts from a profile in the project's dataset: 1,000 synthetic users, each with 24 months of category-wise expenses.

Read this month's bank SMS

A CNN+GRU classifier separates transactions from OTPs and promotions, a regex extractor pulls out the amount and merchant, and a second CNN+GRU model tags what the money was spent on.

Forecast next month's spending

A per-category ARIMA model projects each spending series; a Random Forest then corrects it using income, the profile and what is on the calendar.

Plan the month's savings, and see why

A reinforcement-learning policy suggests how much of the money left after expenses to save. The amount is blended with your goal deadlines and, from the second month on, with what you actually managed to save.
